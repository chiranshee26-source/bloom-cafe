Since 2019

Our story

It started with one coffee cart, one very old espresso machine and a lot of optimism.

“We wanted a place where the coffee is serious, but nobody else has to be.” — Maya & Jonah, founders

Bean & Bloom began in 2019 as a tiny coffee cart outside Green Town station. Maya roasted the beans in her garage; Jonah baked the croissants in a kitchen that was definitely too small for it.

The queue got longer, the regulars got friendlier, and in 2021 we moved into the old flower shop at 42 Maple Street (that's where the "bloom" comes from).

Today we roast every week, bake every morning, and still know most of our regulars by name. Pull up a chair.

How we got here

The timeline

2019

The little cart

One espresso machine, two founders and a striped umbrella outside the station.

2020

Garage roasting

We started roasting our own beans in small batches, and never looked back.

2021

A real home

We moved into the old flower shop on Maple Street. We kept the name, the plants and the tiles.

2023

The bakery opens

A proper oven, a 5am alarm and the almond croissant that started a small obsession.

Today

Still brewing

12 staff, 3 house roasts and more than 1,200 kind reviews. Thank you for every single cup.

What we care about

Our values

01

Small-batch, always

We buy directly from small farms and roast every week, so your coffee is never more than a few days old.

02

Baked, not bought

Every pastry is made in our own kitchen, starting at 5am. Yes, even the cinnamon rolls.

03

Everyone's welcome

Students, laptops, dogs and grandparents. Oat milk costs nothing extra, and you can stay as long as you like.
