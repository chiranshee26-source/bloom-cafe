Good to know

F.A.Q

Can I work on my laptop there?

Absolutely. There's free wifi, lots of plug sockets along the back wall and a quiet corner upstairs.

Do you have dairy-free options?

Yes! Oat, almond and soy milk are all free of charge, and several of our bakes are vegan. Look for the green badge on the menu.

Are dogs allowed?

Well-behaved dogs are very welcome. Biscuit, our café dog, will probably come say hello.

Can I book the café for a private event?

We host small events (up to 30 people) on weekday evenings. Send us a message with the "Events" topic and we'll get back to you.
